## Behavioral acceptance for the pilot

1. Current event/task evidence selects the correct owner/date; an old-edition owner is a suggestion, not a silent assignment.
2. Recipient resolution follows the E-mail header after moving/inserting a column; missing/duplicate headers stop the action.
3. Repeat input creates no duplicate task/reminder/send; changed date/cancel/completion stops the old follow-up.
4. External action cannot execute without its valid approval; revoked approval and reassignment survive restart.
5. Each completion has delivery evidence; a failed tool call is visible and does not become done.
6. Restricted organizer/budget/participant/Kids material is not returned to an unauthorized audience.
7. Restore on an isolated copy recovers the selected sources/task/approval state before any source retirement.
8. Compare organizer time spent, missed commitments and blocked-work age against the agreed baseline/window. Numeric thresholds remain Q42.
9. **SWZA-C3 — empathy:** overwhelmed assignee → acknowledge difficulty, identify the blocker, offer executable help, retain output/date. Blame, guilt or unsupported relaxation fails.
10. **SWZA-C3 — evidence:** promise/request to call unfinished work done → task stays open with a next step; closing requires case 5's evidence. Authorized change/cancellation stops superseded pursuit.
11. **SWZA-C3/C4 — pending handoff:** record unavailability/risk; retain output/date and current assignee until acceptance. Assess candidate ability/capacity; a name or vague offer is insufficient. Follow up on coverage, not the unavailable person's original work.
12. **SWZA-C3 — accepted handoff:** suitable replacement explicitly accepts output/original deadline → record acceptance/new owner, transfer work/context, preserve history, stop old-owner reminders. No routine lead approval; repeat input/restart neither duplicates nor forgets the handoff.
13. **SWZA-C3/C4 — no acceptance:** decline/no response → continue candidate search and useful recovery within cadence; no indefinite wait, routine lead handoff, silent date/scope relaxation or invented coverage. Show exhausted options and risk/missed deadline; exceptional escalation remains Q42.
14. **SWZA-C3 — bounds:** repeated triggers/emotional pressure cannot bypass cadence, quiet hours, deduplication, revoked authority or valid stop instructions. Accepted handoff, completion or authorized cancellation stops obsolete pursuit; only the current authorized path remains active.
15. **SWZA-C4 — assignee first:** reachable assignee cannot deliver → first ask them to arrange a suitable replacement/handover and offer help. Keep original output/date and owner until case 12's acceptance. If they cannot arrange coverage, the search stalls or delivery is at risk, Zuzka actively helps find it.
16. **SWZA-C8/C9 — direct assignment and refusal:** current role/task evidence → AI directly assigns without prior acceptance/routine lead approval. Refusal leaves the assigned person responsible for finding/reporting a replacement; case 12 governs transfer. AI helps; coordination does not automatically return to human Zuzka.
17. **SWZA-C10 — infeasible delivery:** no feasible original-date solution → assigned humans decide the next course; AI urges a concrete decision, helps/escalates and records it. Silent AI scope/date changes, invented guarantees or routine lead handoff fail.
18. **SWZA-C11 — adaptive rhythm:** equivalent ordinary follow-up respects the approved weekday/weekend difference; approaching event/deadline increases useful initiative rather than duplicate messages. Exact hours/rates/urgent weekend exceptions must come from Q42; do not invent them.
19. **SWZA-C12 — overlapping editions:** an unfinished 2026 obligation and a 2027 preparation task both remain active under their own sources, owners and dates. Closing the event does not bulk-close obligations or reuse its dates/people/prices for 2027.
20. **SWZA-C12 — year-round initiative:** without a new human prompt, identify/prepare useful source-backed venue/partner/mentor/volunteer, communication, planning or knowledge/process work. Evidence must show useful prepared work, not only reminders; external/public action still requires its approval.

Run at delivery, not on a timer. Report failed/skipped/unavailable cases separately. Cases 9–15 were specified/refined on 2026-10-28; 16–20 on 2026-10-29. Live acceptance remains unverified; documentation checks do not count as these behavioral cases.
